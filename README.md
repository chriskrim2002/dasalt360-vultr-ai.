## 📖 Project Overview
**Dasalt 360 Ltd** is a multimodal AI agentic system designed to manage the high-complexity lifecycle of IT hardware in Jimeta, Adamawa State, Nigeria. 

Operating in a volatile economic environment, the system provides **PhD-level financial analysis** to bridge the gap between global procurement (USA), national wholesale markets (Lagos), and local retail distribution (Yola).

### Key Innovation
The system doesn't just process data; it **coordinates and executes**. It handles voice commands from the warehouse, analyzes hardware photos from the field, and performs complex Naira (₦) costing logic to ensure 100% business profitability.

---

## 🚀 Future-of-Work Workflows
1. **Financial Auditor (The CFO Agent):** Performs autonomous landing cost calculations (Rate: ₦1,400/$1) and adds localized logistics fees (₦7,000 per unit transit) to fix profitable retail prices.
2. **Asset Lifecycle Agent:** Uses **Llama 3.2 Vision** to identify laptop generations and automatically triggers 20% clearance discounts for older G1-G5 stock.
3. **Multimodal Interface:** Full **Voice-to-Voice** and **Vision-to-Text** capabilities, allowing hands-free warehouse operations via Vultr Serverless Inference.
4. **PhD-Level Reporting:** Every response is curated in sophisticated, organized Natural Language, befitting a high-level executive dashboard.

---

## 🏗️ Architecture
- **Brain:** Llama 3.1 70B & Llama 3.2 11B Vision (via Vultr Serverless Inference).
- **Backend:** FastAPI (Python) hosted on a **Vultr Dedicated CPU VM**.
- **Logistics Integration:** Real-time coordination with Glorious Lane Ltd tracking APIs.
- **Voice Engine:** Local Speech-to-Text processing combined with Vultr Cloud TTS.
